How It Works
Feed Mode: Scans your past 3 months of upvotes, comments, and posts to construct a personal taste vector, recommending unread articles tailored to your interests.
Username Mode: Looks up any Hive user to display their authored posts at the top, alongside personalized content recommendations based on their activity.
Search Term Mode: Indexes and searches Hive content using corpus-wide IDF weighting to score and rank articles matching your keywords or phrases rapidly.
New Users Mode: Discovers recent articles written by accounts that joined Hive within the last 12 months, with optional keyword filtering.
KE Ratio: Evaluates author sustainability on Hive (Rewards / HP) displayed on every preview and reader view.
Hive Keychain Vote Status: Connect your account via Hive Keychain to automatically see a badge on every post showing whether you have already voted on it.
