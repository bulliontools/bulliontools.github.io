How It Works
Feed Mode: Scans your past 24 months of upvotes, reblogs, comments, and posts to construct a personal taste vector, recommending unread articles tailored to your interests.
Username Mode: Looks up any Hive user to display their authored posts at the top, alongside personalized content recommendations based on their votes, reblogs, and activity.
Search Term Mode: Searches Hive posts and 3Speak videos for your search term (never treating search terms as a username), orders results by highest relevance (newest first on ties), and boosts matches with your reblogs, comments, and optional voting history (up to a 6-month window when Newest is selected).
New Users Mode: Discovers recent articles written by accounts that joined Hive within the last 12 months, with optional keyword filtering.
KE Ratio: Evaluates author sustainability on Hive (Rewards / HP) displayed in author profiles.
Hive Keychain Vote Status: Connect your account via Hive Keychain to automatically see a badge on every post showing whether you have already voted on it.
Anti-Phishing Shield: Intercepts outbound links in posts and comments against known lookalike domains and HP-weighted on-chain phishing flags, with frontend overrides and user opt-out.
